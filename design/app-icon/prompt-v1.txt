Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final square mobile app icon artwork for WashMyCar, a personal car-care planning and Apple Watch step-guidance application.
Primary request: create one original, exceptionally refined, minimalist luxury app icon, combining a water droplet and a car roofline into ONE unified symbol. This is a precise, calm personal detailing companion; the aesthetic should feel considered and enduring.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed deep ink-petrol green field, based on the project's existing #163E3C, with only an almost imperceptible tonal depth. The background extends to all four square edges.
Subject: one substantial, elegantly tapered satin platinum-white droplet emblem. A single generous negative-space incision through its lower-middle describes the flowing roof, windscreen and bonnet of a car in side profile. The car is an abstract smooth contour integrated into the droplet, not a separately drawn car. Keep the droplet readable and the car cue discoverable at small size. Use a broad, confident silhouette and clean optical balance.
Style/medium: precision luxury identity design, vector-clean contour with very restrained satin silver shading. Subtle material elegance, not a glossy chrome render. Silver-white is inspired by the silver car already used in the product.
Composition/framing: front-on, perfectly centered single emblem filling approximately 57 percent of the square width and 61 percent of the square height, generous surrounding negative space, crisp edges, excellent legibility at 60 pixels. Deliver the actual flat square icon asset, 1:1 composition, ideally 1024 by 1024 or larger.
Lighting/mood: quiet, tactile, composed, impeccable, understated.
Color palette: deep ink-green #163E3C, soft platinum and warm pearl-white #F7F8F5.
Text: none.
Constraints: exactly one unified emblem, no typography, no letters, no words, no watermark, no border, no inset tile, no pre-rounded outer corners, no presentation board or device mockup. Do not show realistic cars, wheels, headlights, bottles, tools, foam, bubbles, sparkles, shields, leaves or extra decorative elements. No gold. No imitation of any automaker logo. Keep the square opaque.
